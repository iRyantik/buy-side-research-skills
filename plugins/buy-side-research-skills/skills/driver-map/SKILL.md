# Driver Map

Decompose revenue margin backlog price volume mix and segment drivers before modeling.

## Research Runtime Capsule

- Hook-enforced legality, source boundary, structure floor, and table rendering rules live in workspace hooks and are not restated here.
- Shared runtime/source baseline lives in `skills/_shared/research-policy-baseline.md` and the installed workspace `CLAUDE.md`.
- This skill is a `Primer`. It must preserve `Foundation Layer -> Interpretation Layer -> Research Layer -> Decision Layer`.
- It is a driver-structure skill, not a full company intro and not a valuation note.

## Layer Contract

### Foundation Layer
- what the driver stack is
- how revenue, margin, backlog, or price-volume-mix actually connect

### Interpretation Layer
- which drivers matter most now
- what is most often misread in the reported buckets
- where the reported structure diverges from business reality

### Research Layer
- what sensitivity or bucket still needs validation
- whether the next step should be modeling, consensus work, or more mechanism work

### Decision Layer
- whether the driver map is now good enough for deeper modeling or thesis work

## Quality Bar

Good output should stop the reader from asking:
- what really drives revenue or margin
- whether backlog actually turns into revenue the way management implies

It should push the next question toward:
- which input matters most to the model
- which bucket is masking the real economics
