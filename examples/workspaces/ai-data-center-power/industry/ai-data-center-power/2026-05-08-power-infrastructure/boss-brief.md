# AI Data Center Power Infrastructure

## Conclusion

AI 数据中心电力基础设施是可研究主题，但第一层应该聚焦能把需求直接转化成 revenue、orders、backlog 或 margin 的 electrical / thermal / power equipment 公司，而不是泛化成“所有电力股”。本轮最干净的 direct exposure 是 VRT，其次是 ETN；GEV、CAT、GNRC 更适合作为 read-through。

## Takeaways

1. **VRT 是最纯的 public comp**
   VRT Q1 2026 organic net sales +23.3%、adjusted operating profit +57.0%，并把 FY2026 organic sales growth 指引上调至 +29% 到 +31%。这说明 AI / data-center infrastructure demand 已经进入 reported revenue 和 profit，而不是只停留在主题层面。Source: [Vertiv Q1 2026 results](https://investors.vertiv.com/news/news-details/2026/Vertiv-Reports-Strong-First-Quarter-with-Diluted-EPS-Growth-of-136-Adjusted-Diluted-EPS-Growth-of-83-Raises-Full-Year-Guidance/default.aspx).

2. **ETN 是更均衡的 high-quality comp**
   ETN Q1 2026 Electrical Americas sales +11%、operating margin 29.8%，data center orders 按 TTM 口径 +240%。它没有 VRT 纯，但数据质量和业务韧性更好。Source: [Eaton Q1 2026 results](https://www.eaton.com/content/dam/eaton/company/investor-relations/quarterly-earnings/2026/q1/EatonReportsFirstQuarterEarningsPerShareof$2.45,RecordAdjustedEarningsPerShareof$2.72,Up13PercentOvertheFirstQuarterof2025.pdf).

3. **GEV / CAT / GNRC 不是同质 peer，而是不同 value-chain read-through**
   GEV 偏 generation / grid equipment，CAT 偏 broader machinery 里的 power generation，GNRC 偏 backup power。把它们直接放在同一 peer multiple 里会混淆 driver。

4. **下一层 research edge 在 valuation 和 order quality，不在主题验证**
   对 VRT 而言，关键问题已经从“有没有 data-center exposure”转成“订单、thermal / power mix、margin 和 backlog 是否足以支撑已经抬高的预期”。

## Debate

The bullish case is that AI rack density and data-center growth raise power / cooling content per facility, benefiting VRT and ETN directly. The pushback is valuation and normalization: once market expectations move from “is this real?” to “how long can this growth persist?”, order quality, backlog conversion, and margin bridge matter more than thematic exposure.

## Implications

VRT deserves the next single-name thesis only if the research can reconcile valuation with order durability. If not, ETN may be the cleaner way to underwrite the same theme with lower single-product purity but better diversified quality.
