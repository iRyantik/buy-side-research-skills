# Edge Radar

> This is the English translation of [edge-radar.md](./edge-radar.md). The Chinese version is the source of truth.
>
> This file is the research workspace's problem radar — not a status tracker, not a trade log.

## Usage Principles

- Only record problem types that can change research priorities.
- Do not record raw reminders, unverified inspirations, trade status, or daily todos.
- Every problem should be handoff-ready to a specific skill.

## High-Value Problem Types

| Signal | Typical Symptom | Next Step |
|---|---|---|
| Business reality gap | Disclosure label and real economic substance don't match | `company-history` |
| Know-how gap | Process, equipment chain, engineering principle, or terminology not understood | `mechanism-insight` |
| Driver gap | Revenue / margin / backlog / price-volume-mix not broken down clearly | `driver-map` |
| Consensus gap | Market expectations, priced-in assumptions, or buy-side bar not clear | `consensus-map` |
| Primary evidence gap | Key assumptions need expert call, channel check, survey, or fieldwork verification | `primary-research-plan` |
| Source conflict | Filing, IR deck, call, news, or sell-side source conflicts | `information-impact` |
| Peer mismatch | Market puts company in wrong peer group | `peer-deep-dive` |
| Thesis fragility | Variant view, catalyst, or kill criteria doesn't hold up | `alpha-thesis` / `bear-pre-mortem` |

## Reminder Format

```markdown
**Worth digging deeper**
- Anomaly: [what doesn't look right]
- Could mean: [1–2 explanations]
- Worth asking AI: [1–2 most critical questions]
```
